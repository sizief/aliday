September 2025

Job changing questions

Questions to ask at the time of changing the job. Deal breakers, if answer is no then that's it.
  • Is it fun?
  • Do I believe in the idea or product?
  • Do I want to work the idea or product? Look for what to learn, what to do daily, am i going to use it myself?
  • Are they going to survive? Hard to answer, but looking for high probability NOs, not complicated calculations.
  • Do I like the people? The founders and the team. Looking for diversity and what they believe and the type of work life they follow. Compromising questions - What's the salary, equity, bonus, cliff duration, terms of leaving the company after one year. - working hours, location - work pressure, being oncall, how to promote
